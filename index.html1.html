About

Protecting crops with biology first

I am a plant pathologist based in Ibadan, Nigeria, with a PhD in Plant Pathology from the University of Ibadan. My work covers fungal plant pathogens, crop disease diagnosis, pathogenicity and virulence assessment, molecular characterisation and sustainable disease management, with a focus on biological control agents and plant-derived extracts.

I have worked on diseases of yam and maize, led disease surveys across more than 3,000 oil-palm stands, and teach and mentor students in botany and plant pathology. This site is my online CV and a place to share projects and notes with fellow STEM enthusiasts and with people working on plant health and food safety.

Biological control

Plant extracts and metabolites

Botanical extracts with antifungal activity and the secondary metabolites behind it.

Diagnosis and characterisation of fungal diseases, including postharvest rots of root and tuber crops.

Doctoral research

Lasiodiplodia rot of white yam and its biological control

My thesis characterised Lasiodiplodia species associated with postharvest rot of white yam (Dioscorea rotundata Poir.) and evaluated Trichoderma species and selected botanicals for managing the disease. The work combined morphology, pathogenicity testing and ITS-based molecular analysis with replicated in vitro and in vivo experiments.

Pathogen
Lasiodiplodia spp.
Host
White yam, Dioscorea rotundata Poir.
Characterisation
Colony and microscopic morphology, pathogenicity assays, ITS sequencing and phylogenetic analysis
Management
Trichoderma atroviride, T. harzianum and selected botanical extracts
